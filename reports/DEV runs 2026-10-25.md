# DEV runs of 2026-10-25: noise is larger than the PCA effect

**Two identical DEV runs gave test mIoU 0.559 and 0.538 for the three-month model, and 0.546 and 0.485 for the three-month + PCA model.** Code, data and seed were the same in both runs. The gap therefore measures run-to-run noise, and that noise is as large as the effects the project is trying to compare. Both runs used the older 38-cell notebook. The improvements planned on 2026-10-24 (EMA, cosine schedule, Lovász loss, seasonal statistics, terrain) have not been tested yet.

## Setup

- Model: U-Net with an ImageNet ResNet-34 encoder. Input: Sentinel-1 VV, VH and VV−VH in dB.
- Variants: `three_month` (9 channels) and `three_month_pca` (9 channels + 6 PCA components of the yearly series).
- DEV mode: 15 epochs, 2500 samples per epoch, validation on 3 months, test on all 12 months.
- Data: 176 sites, 1958 / 327 / 302 train / val / test patches. Test sites are never used for training or selection.

## Findings

1. **Noise.** Repeating the same run moved test mIoU by 0.02-0.06 and single-class IoU by up to 0.24 (water, wetland). The rare classes in the test set come from one or two sites each, so one site decides their score.
2. **PCA helps vegetation, not the overall score.** In both runs PCA raised val mIoU by about 0.10, and raised test IoU for trees (+0.13 to +0.14) and shrub & grass (+0.10 to +0.12). Overall test mIoU fell in both runs, mainly through cropland and wetland. The larger train-val gap with PCA (0.127 vs 0.054) suggests that the yearly summary partly memorises site-specific patterns.
3. **Training was not finished.** The best epoch was 14 of 15 in every case. Fifteen epochs are enough to find crashes, not to rank variants.
4. **Weak classes stay weak.** On the test sites chosen for them, shrub & grass reaches 0.03 mIoU, snow 0.15, trees 0.18 and wetland 0.22. Built-up (0.94 IoU) and bare ground dominate the overall score.
5. **Selection leak.** The notebook picked the "best" variant by test mIoU. Model selection must use validation only.

## Consequences

- Choose variants by validation mIoU over all 12 months, with the full 60-epoch budget.
- Run 2-3 seeds for the final variants, or report the noise level measured here next to every comparison.
- Run the 41-cell notebook next. Its seasonal-statistic channels and terrain channels target exactly the classes where PCA fails.
